According to social psychologists, individuals who belong to one’s own group, as well as those associated with highly esteemed and approachable groups, more often elicit admiration or respect compared to those who belong to less esteemed or perceived-as-distant groups. New brain research shows that the emotions people evoke depend not only on the social group, but also on the emotional expression and the distance from the viewer.
